OUR STORY

Travel starts long before take-off.

AEROVA is a flight-booking website concept designed to make exploring routes and comparing travel options simple. From discovering a destination to reviewing a flight, every step is designed around clarity.

This project is a front-end prototype created for learning and demonstration. It is not an operating airline or live booking agency.

WHAT GUIDES US

Travel, made thoughtful.

✧

Clarity

Present useful flight information in a simple, easy-to-follow layout.

◎

Make it enjoyable to explore new destinations and popular routes.
